# Northern March Interaction Prototype

## Products
1. Northern March Interaction Prototype: one ruler, one campaign, no succession.
2. Realm Vertical Slice: adds full Realm government, foreign Realm, economy and succession.
3. Medieval Alpha: multiple Realms, hundreds of characters, exploration, ecology, magic and long history.

## Prototype milestones
0. Deterministic simulation, event scheduler, save/load, debug tools.
1. Reality, observation, beliefs, reports and physical communication.
2. Core autonomous NPCs and delegated strategic orders.
3. Court and belief-based Map validation gate.
4. Personal presence and ruler location.
5. Character networks and Houses.
6A. Four population communities with beliefs, memories, grievances and organisations.
7A. Grain-only material economy and physical convoys.
8. Standing policies and institutional precedent.
9. Environment and geography changes.
10. Delegated Earth/Frost magic.
11. Blackwood exploration, monster and Stone of Vey.
12. Arvenese frontier network.
13. Limited-visibility battlefield laboratory.
14. Conversational commands, physical messengers and commander obedience.
15. Chronicle, reconstruction and emergence tests.

## Prototype ending
Anna's death, decisive capture/deposition, loss of the Northern March, collapse of authority, or decisive objective failure ends the campaign. Succession starts only in Product 2.
