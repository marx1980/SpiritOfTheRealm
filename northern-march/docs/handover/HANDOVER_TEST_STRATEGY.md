# Northern March Interaction Prototype

## Four layers
A. Automated mechanical correctness.
B. NPC behavioural experiments and parameter sweeps.
C. Headless campaign batches and counterfactuals.
D. Small, disciplined human sessions.

## Blockers before 6A
Information leak, undelivered information use, broken latency, WorldState planner access, missing decision reconstruction or unexplained deterministic divergence.

## Not blockers
Imperfect balance, incomplete content, UI polish or lack of emergence that depends on communities/resources not built yet.

## Human test rule
Do not teach the philosophy first. See whether players learn that reports and map markers are beliefs with sources and ages.
