# Northern March Interaction Prototype

## Session metadata
Build, date, tester type, scenario/seed, input method and objective.

## Observe
For each consequential moment record player belief, cited evidence, decision, expected result, visible result, confusion and verbatim quote. Tag INFO, AGENCY, NPC, DELAY, TRUST, EMERGE, UNFAIR or DELIGHT.

## Trust map
At opening, midpoint and end ask who the player trusts, distrusts, sees as competent in which domain, and suspects of having an agenda.

## Information mental model
Ask where Varos is, which report is freshest, which source is trusted, what is uncertain, and whether the map shows truth or government belief.

## NPC legibility score
0 random/broken; 1 weak post-hoc explanation; 2 plausible but poorly signalled; 3 player inferred likely cause; 4 player anticipated behaviour from character.

## Agency checklist
At least two approaches, enough evidence to reason, way to seek information, delegation options, risk-management lever, meaningful consequence.

## End questions
What happened? Biggest mistake? Who would you trust next time? What information was missing? What felt unfair/deserved? Any “I knew it” or “should have listened” moment? Could the player express intended commands? Would they replay?
