# Northern March Interaction Prototype

## Canonical scenarios
T01 Stale Scout Report: old belief remains influential without magical updates.
T02 Contradictory Intelligence: source trust and competence shape uncertainty handling.
T03 Bad Royal Order: capable adviser challenges and improves poor intent.
T04 Opportunity Versus Order: reasoned adaptation/disobedience with smooth gradients.
T05 Missing Messenger: old order persists until new one arrives.
T06 Resource Shock: supply change affects plans without directly spawning unrest.
T07 Community Grievance: same requisition helps Northwatch and harms Greyfen.
T08 Emergent Candidate: Stefan rises from background through recommendation/performance.
T09 Controlled Withdrawal: local troops can misread retreat as rout.
T10 Same Reality, Different Beliefs: NPCs form different understandable plans.

## Integrated tests
I01 Grain Crisis: full material-political causal loop.
I02 Two-Community Divergence: opposite interpretations of one shipment.
I03 Mobilisation Without Revolt Meter: severe grievance alone cannot create armed revolt.

## Execution order
T01, T05, T10, T02, T03, T04, T06, then build 6A-7A, then T07, T08 and I01-I03. T09 follows battlefield kernel.
